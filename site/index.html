Cactus Whistle on ESP32

Offline speech recognition on a microcontroller: hold a button, speak, read the transcript on the screen. No network, no operating system. Flash it onto your board from this page.

This page needs the Web Serial API: use Chrome, Edge or Opera on a desktop computer.

1. Pick your board

2. Flash

3. Talk to it

After flashing, the board restarts and is ready in about a second. Hold a pad (VoCat) or a button (Korvo), speak for up to 10 seconds, let go. It recognises English, German, French, Spanish, Italian, Dutch and Polish, and detects which one you spoke. A 2.5 s phrase takes about 15–20 s to transcribe.
